Doron Dunie, member of Interlake High School’s Class of 2002, passed away on April 6, 2026, after complications with cancer treatments. He was 42 years old.

After graduating from Interlake, Doron went to the University of Washington. He built a successful career in product management, found the love of his life in his wife, Betina, and became a father to his son, Berel. His family and friends were always at the center of his life.

Doron will be remembered for his kindness, unwavering loyalty, and passion for life. He was the kind of friend who always showed up for the people he cared about, and his generosity, humor, and enthusiasm left a lasting impression on everyone who knew him.

Doron will be tremendously missed.
