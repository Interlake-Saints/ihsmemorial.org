Donald was born on April 1, 1962. He loved the outdoors, sunshine, fishing, and animals. He worked as a finish carpenter most of his life until his health deteriorated. He was a member of the Sno Valley Eagles 3529 where he had many friends.

Donald passed on Sunday, June 26, 2016. He was 54 years old.

Sadly, Donald is not the only member of his family on this page. His [older sister Kellie passed away in 2026](https://ihsmemorial.org/class-of-1978/kellie-lynn-ekberg/).
