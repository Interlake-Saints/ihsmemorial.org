Kellie, aka Kellie Lynn Pingree, was born May 7, 1960, to Bruce and Karen Ekberg in Seattle. She graduated from Interlake in 1978.

After high school, she moved to Susanville. She attended Lassen Community College, where she earned associate degrees in business management and accounting. In 1998, she moved to Quincy, where she resided until her death. She passed away peacefully at Alpine Skilled Nursing and Rehabilitation Center in Reno on July 6, 2026. She was 66 years old.

Sadly, Kellie is not the only member of her family on this page. Her younger brother [Donald passed away in 2016](https://ihsmemorial.org/class-of-1980/donald-bruce-ekberg/).
